📘 How It Works

Fixlo’s Expense Tracker is designed for simplicity and privacy. All your financial data is stored locally in your browser using modern web storage — not on our servers.

  • 💡 Add transactions easily with amount, category, and date.
  • 📊 View totals by month and category to identify spending trends.
  • 🧾 Export data to CSV anytime for your own record keeping.
  • 🔒 No sign-in required — your data stays private and secure.

💡 Why Tracking Matters

Understanding your financial habits is the first step toward better budgeting. By visualizing your spending behavior, you can make informed decisions and set achievable savings goals. Fixlo helps you build this awareness in an intuitive, distraction-free way.

🔒 Privacy & Data Security

Your financial records never leave your browser. Fixlo uses secure, local data storage methods and does not transmit or collect any user data.

We respect your privacy — no login, no tracking, and no hidden analytics. You control your data at all times.
