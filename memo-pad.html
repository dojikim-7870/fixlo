📘 How It Works

The Fixlo Memo Pad is built for simplicity. It stores your notes in your browser using secure local storage — not on any external server. This means your writing stays private, fast, and fully under your control.

  • Write instantly without signing up or logging in.
  • Your notes are saved automatically and persist locally.
  • Export your notes as a text file whenever you need.
  • No internet connection required after loading the page.

💡 Best Uses

  • Jot down ideas, plans, or to-do lists quickly.
  • Keep study notes or daily journal entries in one place.
  • Use it alongside Fixlo’s Pomodoro Timer for focused writing sessions.
  • Store temporary text or copy-paste snippets during work.

Whether you’re a student, professional, or creative, the Memo Pad adapts to your workflow effortlessly.

🔒 Privacy & Security

All notes are stored locally in your browser. Fixlo does not collect, transmit, or process any user content. Your text remains on your device only — safe and private.

No login, no tracking, no data sharing. This ensures complete confidentiality of your notes at all times.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are my notes automatically saved?

Yes. Every change you make is instantly stored in your browser’s local storage.

Can I access my notes from another device?

No, your notes are saved locally for privacy. You can export them manually if needed.

Is there a size limit for notes?

You can store thousands of characters, but extremely large notes may depend on browser limits.

Does Fixlo collect or analyze my data?

No. Fixlo does not access, collect, or share any personal information or note content.
