Privacy Policy

We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

Privacy Overview

At Fixlo, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

The short version: We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. All tools work locally in your browser.

Information We Don't Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Text content you process through our tools
  • Voice recordings or transcriptions
  • Expense data or financial information
  • Notes, memos, or any content you create
  • Location data or device identifiers
  • Usage patterns or behavioral data

Local Storage

Some tools (Expense Tracker, Memo Pad, Pomodoro Timer) use your browser's local storage to save data for your convenience. This data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers. You can clear this data at any time.

Advertising

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense. These advertising networks may use cookies. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings.

Data Security

Since we don't collect your personal data, there's no risk of your information being compromised in a data breach. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
