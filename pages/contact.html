📬 Get in Touch

At Fixlo, we believe that communication with our users is the key to improvement. If you have any questions, encounter technical issues, or wish to share suggestions, we’d love to hear from you. Our goal is to provide reliable, free, and privacy-focused productivity tools that make your daily work easier.

We read every message carefully and continuously enhance our platform based on real user feedback. Please note that we do not collect or store any personal information submitted through emails. Every inquiry is handled manually by our support team within 24–48 hours during business days.

  • 💡 General Inquiries — Ask us anything about Fixlo or our features.
  • 🛠️ Technical Support — Report bugs, performance issues, or broken tools.
  • 🌟 Feature Requests — Suggest improvements or new tools you'd love to see.

📧 support@fixlo.info

About Our Support

Fixlo’s support team is dedicated to ensuring a positive user experience for everyone. We provide human-driven, personalized responses without the use of automated replies. Our mission is to create a space where your feedback directly shapes future updates of our tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I contact Fixlo support?

You can reach us directly at support@fixlo.info. We usually respond within 24 hours.

How do I report a bug or error?

Include “Bug Report” in your email subject and describe the issue in detail. Screenshots help us fix problems faster.

Are my data and messages safe?

Yes! All Fixlo tools process your data locally. We don’t collect, store, or share any user information.

Can I request new tools or features?

Absolutely! We actively develop new tools based on community suggestions. Your ideas matter to us.

Need further assistance?

We're always happy to help. If your inquiry requires special attention, please include details in your message for a faster response.

Response Time

We typically respond within 24–48 hours during business days.

User Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. Emails are used solely for communication and never stored in databases or shared with third parties.
