About Fixlo

Fixlo is your all-in-one productivity toolkit — a collection of free, browser-based tools designed to help you focus, create, and organize more efficiently.

Empowering Productivity
Through Simplicity & Privacy

🎯 Our Mission

Fixlo was created with a simple mission — to make productivity tools accessible to everyone, everywhere. We believe powerful digital utilities shouldn’t be locked behind paywalls or privacy-invasive apps. Our team is dedicated to creating tools that empower users to work smarter while keeping their data secure and private.

We focus on simplicity, reliability, and accessibility. Every tool is built to perform quickly, require no sign-up, and work directly in your browser without storing your personal data.

🚀 Our Tools

Fixlo currently provides six essential productivity tools — each crafted to simplify your workflow and save time. Here’s what you can do with them:

🔊 Text to Speech — Convert any text into natural-sounding audio with voice, pitch, and speed options. Great for multitasking, learning, or accessibility.
🎤 Speech to Text — Transcribe spoken words in real-time with high accuracy. Perfect for meetings, interviews, or quick notes.
📝 Text Summarizer — Condense long articles and documents into concise summaries while keeping key information intact.
💰 Expense Tracker — Manage your spending with clear visuals, categories, and trends. Helps you take control of your finances.
⏰ Pomodoro Timer — Stay focused with the Pomodoro technique. Customize work/break sessions and track your daily productivity.
📋 Memo Pad — Take quick notes that auto-save instantly in your browser. Simple, fast, and secure for jotting ideas.

Each tool is free, fast, and privacy-friendly — no accounts, no data collection, just pure productivity.

🤖 Our Technology & Privacy Commitment

Fixlo runs entirely in your browser using modern web APIs. This means no user data ever leaves your device. We use no external databases or tracking systems for any of our tools.

Our tools are built with lightweight JavaScript and optimized for performance. We constantly test across browsers to ensure speed and reliability. Your data privacy and browsing safety are our top priorities.

📈 Future Plans

We’re continuously improving Fixlo and plan to expand our platform with new tools such as document converters, grammar enhancers, and collaboration utilities. Our long-term goal is to build the most trusted ecosystem of privacy-centered productivity applications.

If you have ideas or want to collaborate, feel free to contact us anytime at support@fixlo.info.
