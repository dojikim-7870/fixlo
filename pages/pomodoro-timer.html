The Pomodoro Technique divides your workflow into focused intervals — usually 25 minutes of work followed by a short break. This structured timing system helps increase focus and prevent mental fatigue.

📘 How It Works

Each Pomodoro cycle consists of:

  • Focus Session: 25 minutes of uninterrupted work.
  • Short Break: 5 minutes to rest your mind and stretch.
  • Long Break: After every 4 sessions, take a longer break (15–30 minutes).

This simple rhythm helps you stay productive while avoiding burnout. Fixlo’s timer automatically alternates between sessions and breaks for you.

🎯 Benefits of Using a Pomodoro Timer

  • Improves concentration and minimizes distractions.
  • Encourages healthy breaks and consistent workflow.
  • Helps measure actual productive time vs. idle time.
  • Builds long-term focus discipline through repetition.

Even small, consistent Pomodoro sessions can significantly improve your work output and personal time management.

💡 Productivity Tips

  • Keep notifications off during focus sessions.
  • Use breaks to stretch, hydrate, or take a short walk.
  • Track how many Pomodoros you complete daily — aim for gradual improvement.
  • Pair with Fixlo’s Memo Pad to jot down distractions quickly.

🔒 Privacy & Local Operation

Fixlo’s Pomodoro Timer runs entirely within your browser. No login, no tracking, and no data collection — your settings are saved locally on your device. You can safely manage your work sessions without privacy concerns.
