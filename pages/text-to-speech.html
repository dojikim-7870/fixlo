Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a character limit for text-to-speech conversion?

No, you can convert unlimited text. However, very long texts may take longer to process depending on your browser's capabilities.

Can I download the audio file?

Currently, the tool plays audio in real-time. We're working on adding download functionality in a future update.

Which languages are supported?

The available languages depend on your browser's speech synthesis capabilities. Most modern browsers support multiple languages and accents.

Does this work offline?

Yes! The text-to-speech functionality works completely offline as it uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis.
