📘 How It Works

Our summarization engine uses advanced linguistic analysis to evaluate sentence importance, context relevance, and keyword frequency. It mimics how a human might identify main ideas while eliminating unnecessary filler text.

  • Analyzes text structure and key phrases
  • Ranks sentences based on importance
  • Extracts the top-ranked information
  • Reconstructs the output in clear, natural language
